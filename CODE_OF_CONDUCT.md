# Code of conduct

We want everyone who takes part in this project to have a respectful, harassment-free experience, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use the project;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to [support@anosys.ai](mailto:support@anosys.ai). Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
